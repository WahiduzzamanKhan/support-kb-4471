parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Day-first tenants write 03/05 for 3 May; the parser reads 5 March. Day and month swap. This is what #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for this.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. A day-first tenant’s people type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Same symptom, different code: the parser fix does not touch it. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here. #4471’s own table already showed this row as “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”.

one-day: dates move by one day

No swap: Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made. That is a time-zone shift, not a date order. The desk had already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but counted them under parser.

For the desk

  • Before you merge a report into a ticket, check its cause, not only its subject: the explorer on triage shows how.
  • A report about a one-day move does not belong with a day/month swap.
  • When the parser fix ships, keep the hint and one-day tickets open.
